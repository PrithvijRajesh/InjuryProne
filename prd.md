# PRD

## Project Name

InjuryProne

## User

Athlete or avid gym lifter who gets injured or is sore all the time.

## Problem

Many athletes and lifter deal with injuries or soreness. Without the proper care for the injury, the injury can become really impactful. The users need a way to quickly diagnose their injuries and soreness and understand what steps they can take to recover and prevent it in the future.

## Core User Story

As a user I can click on a muscle group on the diagram to see the muscles in that group and then click on a specific muscle to see what injury I might have based on symptoms and best practices to recover and prevent the injury.

## Features

1. User can click on muscle group on diagram and see symptom questions for that muscle group.
2. User can click on their symptoms and click on one or multiple symptoms
3. User can click submit to get potential diagnosises.
4. User can click on the injuries to see how they can recover from the injury and prevent it in the future

## Out of Scope

1. Connecting to user's health apps
2. Connecting to user's health insurance portals
3. Injuries that are not physical such as psychological injuries and chemical or environmental injuries
4. Giving treatment recommendations such as suggesting specialist doctors close by or physical therapy places close by, but it can give generic recommednations such as suggesting seeing doctor or physical therapy.

## Data We Store

Only data about injuries if we store them in a database
